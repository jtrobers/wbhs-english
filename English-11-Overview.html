Welcome to
English 11

English 11 is about more than reading stories. It is about understanding people, ideas, choices, and the world around us.

Formative

Activity Overview

Welcome to English 11! This first activity introduces the course, classroom expectations, digital organization, and the themes we will explore throughout the year.

Students will review the syllabus, learn how to store class files in OneDrive, explore the course website, and complete the required first-week acknowledgements.

Essential Question

How can reading, writing, and discussion help us better understand ourselves and the world around us?

Why English 11?

Literature helps us understand people, perspectives, and the choices that shape our lives. Throughout this course, students will read stories that challenge assumptions, examine difficult questions, and encourage thoughtful discussion.

The goal is not simply to finish books. The goal is to become stronger readers, writers, thinkers, and communicators.

What We'll Explore This Year

Choices & Consequences

Big Question: How do our choices shape who we become?

Featured Texts: Long Way Down, A Retrieved Reformation

Fear, Rumors & Hysteria

Big Question: What happens when fear becomes more powerful than facts?

Featured Texts: Monsters Are Due on Maple Street, The Lottery, The Crucible, I, Tituba

Reading, Writing & Discussion

Students will read fiction and nonfiction, write with evidence, participate in discussion, revise their work, and support ideas with reasoning.

Skills We'll Practice

Making Claims Finding Evidence Explaining Reasoning Character Analysis Theme Development Discussion Perspective Author's Craft

Learning Targets

  • I can explain the purpose of English 11.
  • I can understand classroom expectations.
  • I can navigate the English 11 course website.
  • I can organize my English 11 files in OneDrive.
  • I can identify the major themes we will explore this year.

Materials

  • Course Syllabus
  • Chromebook
  • Pencil or pen
  • OneDrive access

Student Directions

  1. Explore the English 11 course website.
  2. Review the course overview and classroom expectations.
  3. Read the syllabus.
  4. Set up your English 11 OneDrive folder.
  5. Complete the syllabus acknowledgement.
  6. Complete the OneDrive acknowledgement.
  7. Ask questions so you know how to be successful in English 11.

Teacher Notes

Purpose

This lesson introduces students to the structure, expectations, and purpose of English 11 while making sure they are prepared to use the digital resources they will need throughout the year.

Suggested Flow

  • Welcome and opening message
  • Website tour
  • Course overview
  • Classroom expectations
  • OneDrive setup
  • Syllabus review
  • Acknowledgements and questions

Discussion Starters

  • What makes a story memorable?
  • Why do people tell stories?
  • What makes a respectful discussion?
  • What skills do you hope to improve this year?

Welcome to the Journey

Throughout this year, students will encounter characters facing difficult decisions, communities struggling with fear, and authors who challenge the way we think.

Students will read stories that ask important questions, support ideas with evidence, and consider perspectives that may be different from their own. Welcome to English 11.
